Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information tradeshowboothdesigner.com collects and how it is used. It applies to this website only.

Information we collect

When you submit a form on this website — including the contact form and the free booth design request form — we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, company, email address, phone number, upcoming trade show details, booth size, project notes, and any file you choose to upload.

How we use this information

Information submitted through our forms is used to respond to your inquiry, prepare a design concept or quote, and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Form processing

Form submissions on this site are processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service, which transmits submissions to us by email. Please refer to Web3Forms' own privacy documentation for details on how they handle data in transit.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers. If that changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Data retention

We retain information submitted through our forms for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and, where applicable, deliver services you've requested.

Your choices

You can request that we delete information you've submitted by contacting us using the details on our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
