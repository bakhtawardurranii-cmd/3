Custom Trade Show Booth Displays

Custom trade show booth displays: exhibit, modular, or portable?

Custom trade show booth displays cover a few very different approaches — each suited to a different exhibiting pattern. Getting this choice right matters as much as the design itself.

Request Free Consultation Ask Which Fits Your Show

Two different things

Booth design and booth display aren't the same decision.

Booth design is the planning work — layout, visitor flow, branding and messaging, covered on our custom booth design page. Booth display is the physical structure that design gets built into.

Design decisions usually come first. Once you know what the space needs to do, it's much easier to tell whether a fully custom exhibit, a modular system, or a portable display is the right structure to build it on.

CUSTOM EXHIBIT

A structure designed and built specifically for your brand and space — the most flexible, least reusable option.

MODULAR DISPLAY

A system of reusable components that can be reconfigured into different booth sizes across multiple shows.

PORTABLE DISPLAY

Lightweight, fast to set up and easy to ship — built for frequency and simplicity over full customization.

Comparing your options

How the three display types actually differ.

Making the call

A few questions worth answering first.

01

How many shows per year?

A single flagship show can justify a fully custom exhibit. A busy show calendar usually favors a modular or portable system that travels well and resets quickly.

02

Does your footprint change?

If you exhibit in different booth sizes across shows, a modular system built to reconfigure will hold up better than a fixed custom structure.

03

Who's setting it up?

Professional install crews open up more complex, custom builds. If your own team is handling setup, portable or modular options are usually more realistic.
