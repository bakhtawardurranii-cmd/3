Free Booth Design Consultation

A free starting point for your next trade show booth.

Share a few details about your upcoming show and booth space, and we'll put together an initial design concept — a low-risk way to see our design direction before committing to a full project.

What's included

What you can expect from the free consultation.

  • An initial layout concept based on your booth size and goals
  • A starting point for visitor flow and zoning
  • A conversation about which display approach fits your show calendar
  • No obligation to move into paid design services

What's not included

  • Final production-ready design files
  • Unlimited revisions
  • Fabrication, printing or booth production

Those come with a full design engagement, which we'll discuss after the free concept if it's a fit for your project.

Who it's for

Companies preparing for an upcoming trade show — whether that's a first exhibit, a replacement for a rented display, or a re-design of a booth that hasn't been performing.
