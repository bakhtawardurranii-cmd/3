About

A design studio built around one specialty: trade show booths.

Trade Show Booth Designer focuses on custom trade show booth design and exhibit design — not general branding, not printing, not booth rental logistics. Just the design work that determines whether a booth actually performs on the show floor.

Our approach

Design decisions come before graphics.

Most booths are designed backwards — graphics first, layout as an afterthought. We start with the floor.

01

Start with the floor plan

Booth dimensions, entry points and adjacent exhibitors shape every other decision. Layout comes before graphics, not after.

02

Design for how people move

Visitor flow determines where a display, a counter or a demo station actually belongs — and where they don't.

03

Say one thing clearly

A booth that tries to communicate everything usually communicates nothing. We help narrow the message to what matters on the floor.

Who we work with

Built for exhibitors planning their next show, not just their first.

We typically work with companies in one of a few situations: preparing for a first trade show and starting from scratch, replacing a rented or generic display with something built around their brand, or re-thinking a booth that hasn't been performing the way it should.

If you're not sure which category you fall into, that's a fine place to start a conversation — the free design concept is built for exactly that.

FIRST SHOW

Starting from a floor space and a set of goals, with no existing booth to work from.

OUTGROWING RENTAL

Moving on from a generic rented or portable display toward something built around the brand.

RE-DESIGN

An existing booth isn't generating the conversations or traffic it should — and the layout is usually why.
