Why design matters

A booth can be attractive and still fail to do its job.

Most booth problems aren't about budget — they're about planning decisions made before the space was ever built.

01

Visitors don't understand what you do

A booth can look sharp from across the aisle and still leave people unsure what the company actually offers. That gap is usually a messaging and layout problem, not a graphics problem.

02

Traffic flow creates bottlenecks

Counters, displays and furniture placed without a flow plan can congest entry points, block sightlines, or push visitors to walk past without stopping.

03

Space gets used inefficiently

A large footprint doesn't guarantee a strong presence. Without deliberate zoning, space gets wasted on areas that don't support conversations or lead capture.
