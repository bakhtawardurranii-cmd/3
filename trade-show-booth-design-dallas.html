Exhibiting in Dallas

A competitive show floor calls for a booth that earns a second look.

Dallas hosts a wide range of trade shows, conventions and industry events throughout the year, drawing exhibitors from across Texas and beyond. On a large show floor, a booth is competing with dozens of neighbors for the same few seconds of visitor attention.

That environment rewards design decisions made early — a layout that reads clearly from the aisle, a message that doesn't require a visitor to stop and decode it, and a flow that gets people into the space instead of past it.

Design considerations for Dallas shows

A few things that consistently matter on Dallas show floors.

01

Large convention floors

Big venues mean big aisles and a lot of competing booths. Sightlines, height and contrast matter more than they do in a smaller regional show.

02

A packed exhibition calendar

Companies exhibiting at multiple North Texas shows a year often benefit from a display approach — modular or portable — that can reset quickly between events.

03

Local logistics

Booth design decisions — materials, size, setup complexity — should account for how the booth will actually be delivered, installed and struck at the venue.
